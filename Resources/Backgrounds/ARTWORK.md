# Original background artwork

Three photo-style backgrounds were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this project, then copied into this directory. No Xnapper artwork is included. Each delivered image is **1586 × 992 pixels**. The prompts requested a larger size; the generated files were kept at their actual native resolution, not enlarged or described as 4K.

Six additional abstract backgrounds (Silk Ribbon, Pastel Mesh, Midnight, Sage Paper, Pearl, Ember) are drawn from original procedural artwork at **3840 × 2400 pixels** in `BackgroundLibrary.swift`. Their thumbnails are separate, smaller renders. The full artwork is used for exports.

Background images may be scaled to fill a larger canvas. This does not resample the screenshot itself: screenshot pixels are rendered separately at native resolution.

## Built-in generation prompts

### Dawn Dunes - dunes.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium desktop wallpaper background used behind a centered screenshot card. Create an original wide 16:10 high resolution 3840x2400 landscape photograph of sculptural desert sand dunes at dawn, elegant flowing curves, warm peach and terracotta sand, subtle violet shadows, very fine natural texture. Minimal composition, low contrast through center so UI card is legible, beautiful softly lit negative space. Full bleed only landscape. No text, no watermark, no logos, no device, no screenshot, no frame.

### Quiet Coast - coast.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium desktop wallpaper behind centered screenshots. Create an original high resolution 3840x2400 wide 16:10 aerial photograph of a tranquil teal ocean and white surf meeting pale cream sand diagonally, soft morning light, quiet minimalist composition, delicate organic water texture. Full bleed, no text, no logo, no watermark, no objects, no people, no device, no UI, no border. Sophisticated calm color palette with aquamarine turquoise and ivory.

### Alpine Mist - mist.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium desktop wallpaper background for screenshot editor. Original wide 16:10 high resolution 3840x2400 landscape photograph of layers of distant alpine mountains emerging through violet and lavender mist at blue hour, extremely calm atmospheric haze, muted pastel lilac sky, naturally detailed silhouettes toward edges, soft low contrast middle for a UI card overlay. Full bleed photograph only, no text, no watermark, no logo, no people, no device, no UI, no border.
